**Create Tiny Clockwork Device**

You can spend 10 minutes casting *Prestidigitation* to create a Tiny
clockwork device:

**AC** 5

**HP** 1

When you create it, you determine its function by choosing one effect
from *Prestidigitation*; the device produces that effect whenever you or
another creature activate it with a Bonus Action. If the chosen effect
has multiple effects, you choose one of those options. For example, if
you choose the spell’s ignite-extinguish effect, you determine whether
the device ignites or extinguishes fire; the device doesn’t do both.

You can have 3 devices in existence at a time, and each falls apart 8
hours after its creation or when you dismantle it with a touch as a
Utilize action.

**Tiny Clockwork Device: Dismantle**

You can take the Utilize action to dismantle the clockwork device.

**Unarmed Strike**

You make a melee attack that involves using your body to deal one of the
following effects:

***Damage.*** You make an attack roll against the creature, and on a
hit, you deal 1 + STR Bludgeoning damage.

***Grapple.*** The target must succeed on a Str./Dex. (it chooses which)
saving throw (DC = 8 + Prof. Bonus + Str.) or it has the Grappled
condition.

***Shove.*** The target must succeed on a Str./Dex. (it chooses which)
saving throw (DC = 8 + Prof. Bonus + Str.) or you can either push it 5
ft. away or cause it to have the Prone condition.

When you finish a Short Rest, you can recover expended spell slots. The
spell slots can have a combined level equal to no more than **10**, and

**Overchannel** (Special)

When you cast a Wizard spell with a spell slot of levels 1–5 that deals
damage, you can deal max damage with that spell on the turn you cast it.

The first time you do, you suffer no adverse effect. If you use this
before you finish a Long Rest, you take **2d12** Necrotic damage for
each level of the spell slot. This damage ignores Resistance and
Immunity. Subsequent uses before finishing a Long Rest increases the
Necrotic damage per spell level by **1d12**.

**Sculpt Spells** (Special)

When you cast an Evocation spell that affects other creatures that you
can see, you can choose a number of them equal to 1 + the spell’s level.
The chosen creatures automatically succeed on their saving throws, and
take no damage if they would normally take half on a successful save.
